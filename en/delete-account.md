# Delete your VetKort account

At VetKort, you have full control over your personal data.

## Delete your account in the app

You can delete your account directly in the VetKort app at any time.

1. Open VetKort.
2. Go to **My Account**.
3. Tap **Delete account**.
4. Confirm the deletion.

When your account is deleted, all associated data will also be permanently deleted from our systems.

This includes:

- Pets
- Journals
- Vaccinations
- Medications
- Documents
- Images
- Weight registrations
- Saved clinics
- Other account-related data
